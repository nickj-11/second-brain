# Managing Member Authority Packet

> [!abstract] 🟡 TL;DR
> A four-document, print-ready packet that evidences **Nichilad Jaime**'s authority as Managing
> Member of **OTL Rental LLC** to banks, title companies, lenders, and vendors. The forms are
> blank — they take effect only once the members fill them in, sign, and have them notarized.

---

## 🧭 Context

Banks and title companies rarely accept a bare statement that someone runs an LLC. They ask for
company-executed proof: a members' resolution, an incumbency certificate with a specimen
signature, and — for real property — a filed statement of authority. This packet is those
documents, formatted for letter paper.

> [!warning] 🟠 These papers evidence authority, they don't create it
> Managing-member authority comes from the **operating agreement** (and in some states the
> articles of organization or a filed statement of authority). If that agreement doesn't already
> designate Nichilad Jaime as managing member, amend it first — then sign these. The members sign
> under penalty of perjury and are responsible for what the documents state.

### Blanks to fill before printing

- State of formation, entity file number, EIN
- Date of the operating agreement, and the date Nichilad Jaime became managing member
- Every current member's name and ownership percentage
- Any limitations on authority (write "NONE" if there are none)
- Principal office address and company contact details for the letterhead
- Legal description of the property, if the Statement of Authority is being recorded

> [!danger] 🔴 Verify the spelling before anyone signs
> The packet is typed **NICHILAD JAIME** and **OTL Rental LLC**. Both must match the operating
> agreement, the government-issued ID, and the Secretary of State's record exactly. A mismatched
> letter gets the packet rejected at the counter.
