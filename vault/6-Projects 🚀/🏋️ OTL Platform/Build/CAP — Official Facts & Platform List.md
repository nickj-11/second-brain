**`programming@crossfit.com`** — all four CAP articles end by pointing here, and the access article

## 🔑 Access

**Via the Affiliate Toolkit — the primary route:**
- CAP is **included with affiliation.** No extra charge from CrossFit. "No extra subscription. No
  extra cost. Just access."
- Affiliate must be **in good standing.**
- Coaches **can** access directly, "with valid credentials and Affiliate Toolkit access."
- Staff are added by invitation through the Toolkit.

**Toolkit showing a status error?** Three things must all be true:
1. Affiliate fees current
2. Trainer credential current **and** meeting the terms of the Affiliate Agreement
3. Affiliate Agreement signed

**Via third-party platforms:** "Setup guides and pricing vary on each platform; please reach out to
the third-party platform directly." CrossFit charges nothing, but **platform rates vary** — the
platform may charge to deliver CAP.

## 🗓️ Release schedule

- **Released every Friday, two weeks in advance**
- Programming Calendar available **at least two weeks ahead**
- Platforms sync **nightly** — per the troubleshooting guidance, "Workout deleted in platform? Wait
  for the nightly sync or re-download"
- Language: **English only** (others in development)

> [!info] 🟦 More runway than I previously recorded
> I had "released by Friday at the latest," from PushPress's page. CrossFit's own answer is **two
> weeks in advance**. That materially helps: a parse-and-review pipeline has a fortnight of slack, not
> a weekend.

## 📦 What CAP includes

- **Daily programming** — seven days/week of varied, scalable CrossFit workouts
- **Lesson plans** — warm-ups, whiteboard briefs, scaling, logistics, cool-downs, coaching resources
- **Stimulus and coaching notes** — coach development and class consistency
- **Daily videos** — coaching considerations, stimulus notes, workout-specific detail
- **Supplemental programming** — "Work Your Weakness": three strength options, skill-focused work, a
  stamina-building session
- **Monthly focus areas** — upcoming events, skill development, mindset work
- **Measurement toggle** — imperial and metric
- **Cardio conversion charts**

### Tracks
| Track | For |
|---|---|
| **Affiliate** | Classic daily group classes |
| **Compete** | Athletes preparing for the Open and beyond |
| **At-Home** | Remote/home training |
| **Masters 55+** | Masters athletes |

*Correction: earlier notes listed "Affiliate, At Home, Compete and Lifting" from SugarWOD's older
marketing copy. CrossFit's current list has **Masters 55+**, not Lifting.*

### CAP vs CrossFit.com
| | Audience | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **CrossFit.com** | General public | Minimal instruction |
| **CAP** | Affiliates only | Full lesson plans, scaling, coaching tools |

## 🤝 Sharing CAP — what CrossFit explicitly permits

From `How-to-Use-and-Share-CAP`:

**For coaches and staff:**
- Add staff to the Toolkit by invitation
- **Coaches can subscribe to CAP's weekly planning email** ← an ingestion route we control
- Save CAP to the mobile home screen as a shortcut

**For members**, verbatim:
> "You can use SugarWOD, BTWB, Wodify, **or another platform** to share programming with members"

> [!tip] 🟣 "Or another platform" is permissive language worth leaning on
> Sharing CAP with our own members through a platform is explicitly sanctioned. Whether our own
> purpose-built app counts as "another platform" is a reasonable reading — and exactly the question to
> put to `programming@crossfit.com` in writing, so the answer is on record before we build.

> support queue. That is a per-platform commercial arrangement — confirming that no per-gym CAP
> credential exists to find or recreate. It also means **any of these 15 can be our CAP pipe**, which
> is the basis of the corrected ingestion plan.
