## Second brain (Obsidian vault)

My second brain lives at `__VAULT_PATH__`. Treat it as a place you actively use, not a folder you
wait to be pointed at.

**Read from it.** When a question touches my life, projects, money, documents, or people, search
the vault before asking me or answering from general knowledge:
`grep -ril "<term>" "__VAULT_PATH__" --include="*.md"`.

**Write to it.** When a piece of work is finished and worth having in six months — research, a
decision and why, a build or debug writeup, an analyzed document — file it into the vault without
being asked, then tell me where it landed. Use the `second-brain` skill; it has the full procedure.

**Follow the house rules.** `__VAULT_PATH__/CLAUDE.md` defines the MOC convention, the callout
colors, and the note anatomy. Read it before writing anything into the vault, and match it exactly.

**Keep the index honest.** Every folder has a `<Folder Name> MOC.md`. When you add, move, or
rename a file, update that folder's MOC in the same edit.

**Never write secrets into the vault** — no passwords, API keys, tokens, or full account numbers.
It syncs to the cloud and to my phone. Last 4 digits are fine.
